# Harvester 2.2

The native queue now accepts individual public media pages and direct file URLs through yt-dlp, in addition to its existing YouTube, Reddit, and local-file adapters. Support depends on the site's extractor and access requirements; this is not universal capture. Collections, live streams, DRM, and authenticated general-page URLs are rejected. No browser cookies are supplied to this general adapter.

Firefox 2.2 adds naming, start/end trimming, video/audio preset selection, immediate Harvest, Add to queue, and Open Harvester for public pages. The legacy visible-media picker still uses Firefox's output settings, rather than the native queue's naming and trimming options.

Harvester > Settings (Command-comma) controls maximum source duration and download size. Defaults remain 60 minutes and 500 MB. The allowed ceilings are six hours and 5 GB (binary units internally). Changes apply when the queue next starts. Limits apply to full sources even for trimmed clips; output size may be substantially greater, especially for all-keyframe video and WAV audio. These are practical guardrails, not a hardware stability guarantee. Instagram's legacy browser workflow retains its own settings.

Outputs remain a silent H.264 MP4 and separate WAV or FLAC audio, with no retained combined original. Completed video can be sent to Chromatron's existing Harvester queue.
