# Licensing status and intent

## Current status

This private repository currently has **no public license**. Copyright law
therefore reserves the normal rights to the copyright holder. Access to the
repository does not grant permission to copy, redistribute, modify, sublicense,
or commercialize the code.

## Intended public-release policy

The project owner intends to consider publishing HARVESTRR's source after the
product is fully operational, while prohibiting commercial reuse and commercial
clones.

That restriction is incompatible with the commonly accepted definition of
"open source": OSI-approved open-source licenses permit use for commercial
purposes. A future release with a noncommercial restriction would be more
accurately described as **source-available** or **noncommercial source**.

Before any public release, choose or commission license text that clearly defines:

- What counts as commercial use.
- Whether individuals, nonprofits, artists, educators, and researchers may use
  and modify the software.
- Whether redistribution and public forks are allowed.
- Whether hosted services, paid support, bundled hardware, sponsorship, or
  donation-supported use counts as commercial activity.
- How contributions are licensed and whether contributor agreements are needed.
- Whether the name and trademarks are governed separately from the source code.

Do not label a future noncommercial release "open source" without resolving this
conflict. Do not add an OSI license now if commercial use is intended to remain
prohibited.

This document records project intent; it is not a substitute for legal advice or
final license text.
